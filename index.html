Jensen notices the places you'd love, as you walk

An always-on agent on the Claude Agent SDK, with a round badge as its face. Team Jensen, Anthropic Partner Basecamp, 13 Nov 2026.

The badge flowJensen interviews you, then pops nearby places up on the round badge. Tap to read why it picked them. The learning brain (Wander)Onboarding that remembers you, a taste model that learns from what you stop for or skip, and an itinerary that replans itself.

How the pieces fit

  1. Onboarding: a few interests, your pace and how long you have. Wander's onboarding, which remembers you on the next trip. iOS app, ios-app branch
  2. Sense: the phone sends where you are. POST /location
  3. Score and decide: every 20 seconds an Agent SDK agent looks at the real places around you, with photos, and taps you only for one that fits. jensen/always-on.ts
  4. Nudge: the badge shows the place as a round photo with its name. A tap shows why Jensen picked it for you. GET /badge/next, badge firmware
  5. Ask and act: ask the badge a follow-up out loud. Jensen answers and can request a table, find tickets or add the visit to your calendar. POST /ask
  6. Learn: going in, skipping, "not my thing" and lingering steer the next picks. Wander's learning loop. POST /feedback
