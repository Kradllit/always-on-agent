Jensen notices places you'd love, and pops them up on your badge

Prototype of the flow. Jensen interviews you in the app, asks for your location, then watches as you walk. When you pass something that fits you, it shows on the badge as a round picture, and a tap tells you why Jensen picked it for you.

About this prototype, and photo credits

A clickable sketch for the team to agree on the flow, not the app. The places are real, and the reasons are written in advance from each place's facts. In the real Jensen, Claude would write each reason from your interview and the place's details. Distances are measured along a straight line.
